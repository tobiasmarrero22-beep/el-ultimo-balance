# Stoppage Time Facts · guiones de los 4 primeros Shorts (para revisar)

Adaptación al inglés de los Shorts de Dato de Tribuna: mismos hechos verificados, reescritos para el público en inglés
(«nil», libras, «caps», «kick-off»). Debajo de cada título, de qué trata en español. Duración estimada con la voz a 1,05.

## 001 · 149–0… and every goal was scored by the team that lost (~38 s)

*149 a 0: el equipo que perdió metió todos los goles (protesta en Madagascar, 2002).*

1. A hundred and forty-nine… nil! The most lopsided scoreline in football history.
2. And all a hundred and forty-nine goals were scored by… the team that lost!
3. Madagascar, two thousand and two. AS Adema versus SO l'Emyrne, a league match.
4. In their previous game, a controversial penalty late on had cost them the title.
5. So how did they protest? From kick-off, they kept shooting into their own net.
6. The punishment: their coach was banned for three years.
7. What's the craziest scoreline you've ever seen? Comment below.

## 002 · Signed for some tracksuits… then he played at two World Cups (~38 s)

*Cascarino: lo ficharon a cambio de ropa de entrenamiento y jugó dos Mundiales.*

1. This club signed a striker… and paid for him with training kit.
2. England, nineteen eighty-two. Tony Cascarino was playing for Crockenhill, a tiny non-league club in Kent.
3. Gillingham wanted him. The fee? Tracksuits and training equipment.
4. Five years later, they sold him to Millwall for… two hundred and twenty-five thousand pounds!
5. And he went on to play at two World Cups for Ireland!
6. Eighty-eight caps and nineteen goals for his country.
7. Which player turned out to be worth way more than he cost? Comment below.

## 003 · How long did the fastest World Cup goal take? You've got 3 seconds (~32 s)

*Quiz: el gol más rápido de un Mundial (Şükür, 11 segundos, 2002).*

1. How long did the fastest goal in World Cup history take? You've got three seconds.
2. Eleven seconds!
3. Hakan Şükür, Turkey, against South Korea, in the two thousand and two World Cup third-place match.
4. Korea kicked off, lost the ball… and Şükür tucked it away.
5. Twenty-four years later, not even the twenty twenty-six World Cup could beat it.
6. Did you get it right? Comment A, B or C… no cheating.

## 004 · 173,850 people… and then, silence (~35 s)

*Maracanazo: 173.850 personas y el silencio después del 2 a 1 de Uruguay.*

1. A hundred and seventy-three thousand, eight hundred and fifty people in one stadium!… And then… silence.
2. Rio de Janeiro, nineteen fifty. Brazil versus Uruguay: the match that decided the World Cup.
3. Brazil only needed a draw.
4. Brazil went one-nil up!
5. But Uruguay turned it around: one-all… and two-one to Uruguay!
6. It's still the biggest crowd ever at a World Cup match.
7. They called it the Maracanazo. What's your country's most painful defeat?
